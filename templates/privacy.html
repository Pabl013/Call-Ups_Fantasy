Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Call-Ups Fantasy is a fantasy football web application developed as part of the SOEN 287 course at Concordia University.

This Privacy Policy explains what information our application collects, how it is used, and how it is stored.

2. Information We Collect

Our application collects the following information:

  • Username and email address.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Fantasy league names and configurations.
  • Team names and team ownership information.
  • Session information.
  • Football player search queries.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Create and manage fantasy football leagues.
  • Store and display fantasy teams.
  • Provide football player search functionality.
  • Maintain user sessions.

4. Data Storage and Security

Account, league, and team information is stored in a database managed using SQLAlchemy.

Passwords are hashed using bcrypt rather than stored as plain text.

The application uses authentication, access controls, and CSRF protection to help protect user information. However, no system can guarantee complete security.

5. Cookies and Sessions

Call-Ups Fantasy uses session cookies to maintain user authentication and store session information.

These cookies support essential application functionality, including account access and league configuration.

6. Third-Party Services

Our application uses API-Football to retrieve football player information.

Player search terms and player identifiers may be sent to this external service to retrieve results. The player search functionality does not include account passwords or email addresses in these requests.

Retrieved player information and search terms may also be cached locally to reduce repeated API requests.

Some pages use external fonts and images. Loading these resources may share technical information, such as your IP address, with third-party providers.

7. Data Retention and User Controls

Account, league, and team information is retained in the application database.

Users can currently update their fantasy team names. Automated account deletion and data export features are not currently available.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as new functionalities are added to Call-Ups Fantasy.

9. Contact

Call-Ups Fantasy is an academic software project developed for SOEN 287 at Concordia University.

A dedicated privacy contact has not yet been established.
